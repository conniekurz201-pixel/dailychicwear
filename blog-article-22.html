Nordstrom Review: Luxury Shopping Experience

June 19, 2026 | 11 min read | Brand Reviews

Nordstrom is synonymous with luxury retail, offering an unparalleled shopping experience that combines high-end fashion with exceptional customer service. Founded in 1901, this Seattle-based department store has become a staple for fashion-forward individuals seeking quality and style. In this review, we'll explore what makes Nordstrom stand out in the world of luxury retail.

About Nordstrom

Nordstrom is an American luxury department store chain that offers a wide range of products including clothing, shoes, accessories, beauty products, and home goods. With over 350 stores across the United States, Canada, and online, Nordstrom is known for its exceptional customer service and curated selection of premium brands.

Overall Rating

★★★★★
4.9/5

Based on 12,000+ customer reviews and our own testing

Pros of Shopping at Nordstrom

Exceptional Customer Service

Nordstrom is famous for its legendary customer service, including free alterations and a hassle-free return policy.

Premium Brands

From designer labels to exclusive collaborations, Nordstrom offers an impressive selection of luxury brands.

Personal Styling

Free personal styling services help you find the perfect outfit for any occasion.

Rewards Program

The Nordy Club offers points, exclusive access, and special perks for members.

Cons of Shopping at Nordstrom

High Prices

Luxury comes at a price - Nordstrom's products are generally more expensive than department stores.

Limited Locations

Nordstrom stores are primarily located in major cities, which can be inconvenient for some shoppers.

Sizing Challenges

Designer sizing can be inconsistent, making online shopping tricky.

Sale Crowds

During major sales like the Anniversary Sale, stores can become very crowded.

Best Categories at Nordstrom

Nordstrom excels in several categories:

Designer Fashion

Nordstrom carries some of the most prestigious designer brands:

  • Gucci: Iconic luxury fashion
  • Chanel: Timeless elegance
  • Prada: Modern sophistication
  • Valentino: Romantic designs

Shoes

Nordstrom has one of the best shoe selections around:

  • Designer heels: Jimmy Choo, Christian Louboutin, Manolo Blahnik
  • Comfortable shoes: Ecco, Clarks, Vionic
  • Athletic wear: Nike, Adidas, Lululemon

Beauty

The beauty department offers luxury brands and personalized service:

  • La Prairie: High-end skincare
  • Chanel Beauty: Iconic makeup and skincare
  • Tom Ford Beauty: Luxury cosmetics

The Nordstrom Anniversary Sale

The Nordstrom Anniversary Sale is one of the most anticipated events of the year:

  • Held annually in July
  • Early access for Nordy Club members
  • Up to 60% off on select items
  • New arrivals at sale prices

Shopping Tips for Nordstrom

Here are some tips to make the most of your Nordstrom experience:

  • Sign up for the Nordy Club rewards program
  • Book a free personal styling appointment
  • Take advantage of free alterations
  • Shop the Anniversary Sale for the best deals
  • Use Nordstrom's price matching policy

Final Verdict

Nordstrom offers a truly luxurious shopping experience that's worth the investment. From the exceptional customer service to the curated selection of premium brands, Nordstrom sets the standard for luxury retail. Whether you're looking for a special occasion outfit or everyday essentials, Nordstrom delivers quality and style.
