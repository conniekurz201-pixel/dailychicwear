Maybelline Review: Drugstore Beauty at Its Best

June 23, 2026 | 10 min read | Brand Reviews

Maybelline has long been a staple in drugstore beauty, offering high-quality makeup at affordable prices. Founded in 1915, this iconic brand has become known for its innovative products and trendsetting formulas. In this review, we'll explore why Maybelline remains a favorite among beauty enthusiasts.

About Maybelline

Maybelline was founded by Thomas L. Williams in 1915 when he created a mascara for his sister Maybel. Today, Maybelline is part of the L'Oreal group and is sold in over 129 countries. The brand is known for its affordable yet high-quality makeup products.

Overall Rating

Pros of Maybelline

Affordable Prices

Maybelline offers high-quality makeup at drugstore prices, making it accessible to everyone.

Wide Availability

Maybelline products are available at drugstores, supermarkets, and online retailers worldwide.

Innovative Products

Maybelline is constantly introducing new products and formulas to keep up with beauty trends.

Inclusive Shade Range

Many Maybelline products offer a wide range of shades to suit different skin tones.

Cons of Maybelline

Some Products Contain Fragrance

Those with sensitive skin may need to check ingredient lists.

Packaging Quality

Some packaging feels flimsy compared to higher-end brands.

Hit-or-Miss Products

Not every product is a winner, so it's important to read reviews.

Limited Skincare

Maybelline focuses primarily on makeup, with limited skincare offerings.

Must-Try Maybelline Products

Maybelline has many iconic products that have stood the test of time:

Foundation

  • SuperStay 24H Foundation: Long-lasting, full-coverage foundation
  • Fit Me Foundation: Lightweight, natural coverage
  • Dream Velvet Foundation: Smooth, velvety finish

Mascara

  • Lash Sensational: Volumizing and lengthening
  • The Falsies: Dramatic, false-lash effect
  • Great Lash: The original drugstore mascara

Eye Makeup

  • Nude Palette: Versatile neutral eyeshadows
  • Line Stiletto Liquid Liner: Precise winged liner
  • Color Tattoo Eyeshadow: Cream-to-powder formula

Lip Products

  • SuperStay Matte Ink: Long-wearing matte liquid lipstick
  • Color Sensational Lipstick: Creamy, pigmented formula
  • Baby Lips: Hydrating lip balm with color

Maybelline Collections

Maybelline regularly releases new collections to keep up with trends:

  • Seasonal Collections: Limited-edition products for spring, summer, fall, and winter
  • Collaborations: Partnerships with influencers and celebrities
  • Trend Collections: Products inspired by runway trends

Shopping Tips for Maybelline

Here are some tips to make the most of your Maybelline shopping experience:

  • Check for sales at drugstores like Walgreens and CVS
  • Read reviews before buying online
  • Look for coupons and promotions
  • Try products in-store before purchasing
  • Sign up for Maybelline's newsletter for exclusive deals

Final Verdict

Maybelline is a brand that delivers on its promise of affordable, high-quality makeup. With its wide range of products and innovative formulas, it's easy to see why Maybelline has remained a drugstore favorite for over 100 years. Whether you're a makeup novice or a beauty enthusiast, Maybelline has something for everyone.
