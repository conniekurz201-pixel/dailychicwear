Altitude Sports: Canada's Outdoor Gear Destination for Adventure-Ready Style

November 4, 2026 | 7 min read | Fashion

When it comes to outdoor gear in Canada, one name stands above the rest: Altitude Sports. For over half a century, this beloved retailer has been outfitting skiers, hikers, campers, and everyday adventurers across the country — and with good reason. Whether you're gearing up for a Rocky Mountain expedition or just looking for a stylish puffer jacket that actually works, Altitude Sports delivers premium brands, expert advice, and Canadian pricing that's hard to beat.

50+ Years of Canadian Outdoor Heritage

Founded in 1972 in Montreal, Altitude Sports began as a small shop for serious skiers and mountaineers. Today, it's grown into one of the largest outdoor retailers in North America — with multiple stores across Quebec, a booming online business, and a reputation for curating only the best gear from the world's top brands. From The North Face and Patagonia to Arc'teryx, Columbia, Salomon, and adidas Outdoor, if it's built for adventure, Altitude Sports has it.

What Makes Altitude Sports Different?

There are plenty of outdoor retailers out there, but Altitude Sports stands out for three reasons:

  • Canadian expertise — They know cold. Their team includes seasoned skiers, hikers, and mountaineers who actually test the gear they sell
  • Curated selection — Every brand and product is hand-picked. No filler, no no-name junk — just proven performers
  • Montreal-inspired style — Function meets fashion. You'll find gear that looks great in the city and performs in the mountains

What's Hot Right Now at Altitude Sports

The current season at Altitude is packed with fall/winter essentials. Here's what's flying off the shelves.

Down Puffer Jackets

When the temperature drops, Altitude's selection of down puffers is unbeatable. Top picks include the The North Face Himalayan Down Parka, the Arc'teryx Thorium AR, and the Columbia Men's Pike Lake II. Prices range from C$150 to C$800 depending on fill power and brand heritage. Shop all puffer jackets at Altitude Sports →

Merino Wool Baselayers

Merino wool is the secret to staying warm without overheating, and Altitude Sports carries the best. Look for icebreaker New Zealand merino, Smartwool baselayer sets, and Black Diamond wool blend tops. Perfect for layering under a jacket or wearing on its own.

Hiking Boots & Trail Runners

Altitude is legendary for its boot selection. Hit the trails in Salomon Speedcross trail runners, Merrell Moab hiking boots, or Asolo backpacking boots. Whether you're tackling the Bruce Trail or heading to Banff, they've got the right fit — and expert boot fitters to help.

Skis, Snowboards & Winter Gear

As winter approaches, Altitude becomes a one-stop shop for skiers and boarders. They carry top brands like Rossignol, Head, Burton, and Lib Tech, plus all the accessories — bindings, boots, helmets, goggles, and more.

Altitude Sports Exclusives

One of the coolest things about Altitude Sports is their in-house and exclusive collaborations. Keep an eye out for the ALX Collective — their own line of premium outdoor apparel designed in Montreal. Also watch for limited-edition drops from brands like Arc'teryx and Salomon that you won't find anywhere else in Canada.

Shopping Tips for Altitude Sports

Here's how to get the best deals at Altitude:

  • Sign up for their newsletter — First-time subscribers get 10% off their first order
  • Shop the Sale section — Year-end and mid-season sales can save you up to 50% on premium brands
  • Free shipping on orders over C$75 within Canada
  • Price match — Altitude will match Canadian competitors' prices
  • Visit a store — In Montreal, Quebec City, or Toronto, you can get expert fitting advice from real outdoor enthusiasts

Final Thoughts

Altitude Sports isn't just a store — it's a Canadian institution. Whether you're an experienced backcountry skier or someone who wants a warm jacket that looks good walking to brunch, they have exactly what you need. The team knows their stuff, the brands are top-tier, and the prices are fair. No wonder they've been Canada's outdoor gear destination for over 50 years. Ready to gear up? Head over to Altitude Sports now to browse their full collection — from puffer jackets and merino baselayers to boots, skis, and everything in between. Your next adventure starts here.
