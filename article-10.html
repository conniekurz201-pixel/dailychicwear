DIY Beauty Recipes for Glowing Skin

June 22, 2026 | 5 min read | Beauty

You don't need expensive products to achieve glowing skin. These simple DIY beauty recipes use ingredients you probably already have at home.

Honey and Yogurt Face Mask

Combine 2 tablespoons of plain yogurt with 1 tablespoon of honey. Apply to clean skin, leave on for 15 minutes, then rinse. Great for hydrating and brightening.

Coffee Scrub

Mix 2 tablespoons of coffee grounds with 1 tablespoon of coconut oil and 1 teaspoon of honey. Gently scrub your face in circular motions, then rinse. Perfect for exfoliation.

Cucumber Eye Pads

Slice a cucumber and place the slices over your eyes for 10-15 minutes. The cooling effect reduces puffiness and dark circles.

Oatmeal Bath Soak

  • 1 cup colloidal oatmeal
  • 1/2 cup Epsom salt
  • 5-10 drops lavender essential oil

Mix ingredients and add to warm bath water. Great for soothing dry, irritated skin.

Green Tea Toner

Brew a strong cup of green tea, let it cool, then apply to your face with a cotton pad. Green tea is packed with antioxidants that protect and brighten skin.

Final Tips

Always patch-test DIY recipes before using them on your face. Store any leftover products in the refrigerator and use within a few days.
