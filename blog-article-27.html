Benefit Cosmetics Review: Fun and Flirty Beauty

June 24, 2026 | 11 min read | Brand Reviews

Benefit Cosmetics is known for its fun, flirty approach to beauty, with products that combine effectiveness with playful packaging. Founded in 1976 by twins Jean and Jane Ford, Benefit has become a favorite among beauty lovers for its innovative products and quirky personality. In this review, we'll explore what makes Benefit stand out.

About Benefit Cosmetics

Benefit Cosmetics was founded in San Francisco by twin sisters Jean and Jane Ford. The brand quickly gained popularity for its innovative products like the "Benetint" lip and cheek stain, which became an instant classic. Today, Benefit is part of the L'Oreal group and operates globally.

Pros of Benefit Cosmetics

Fun Packaging

Benefit products come in colorful, playful packaging that adds joy to your makeup routine.

Innovative Products

Benefit is known for creating unique products that solve specific beauty problems.

Easy to Use

Benefit products are designed to be user-friendly, even for makeup beginners.

Great for Travel

Many Benefit products come in travel-friendly sizes and packaging.

Cons of Benefit Cosmetics

Price Point

Benefit products are more expensive than drugstore brands.

Some Products are Scented

Those with fragrance allergies may need to avoid certain products.

Limited Shade Range

Some products have limited shade options, especially for deeper skin tones.

Hit-or-Miss Products

Not every product lives up to the hype.

Must-Try Benefit Products

Benefit has many iconic products that have become staples in many makeup bags:

Face Products

  • Benetint: The original lip and cheek stain
  • Hoola Bronzer: A cult-favorite matte bronzer
  • Galifornia Blush: A sunny, golden-pink blush
  • POREfessional: A primer that minimizes pores

Eye Products

  • They're Real! Mascara: Lengthening and volumizing
  • Gimme Brow: A tinted brow gel
  • Precisely, My Brow Pencil: A micro-fine brow pencil

Lip Products

  • Benetint: A classic lip stain
  • Ultra Plush Lip Gloss: A moisturizing lip gloss
  • Full Finish Lipstick: Creamy, pigmented lipstick

Benefit Collections

Benefit regularly releases fun, themed collections:

  • Holiday Collections: Special edition products with festive packaging
  • Spring/Summer Collections: Fresh, bright products for warmer months
  • Travel Sets: Mini versions of popular products

Shopping Tips for Benefit

Here are some tips to make the most of your Benefit shopping experience:

  • Visit a Benefit brow bar for a professional brow shaping
  • Sign up for Benefit's rewards program
  • Take advantage of seasonal sales
  • Try travel-sized products before committing to full sizes
  • Check for gift sets during the holidays

Final Verdict

Benefit Cosmetics offers a fun, fresh approach to beauty that's hard to resist. With its innovative products and playful packaging, Benefit adds joy to any makeup routine. While the price point may be higher than drugstore brands, the quality and uniqueness make it worth the investment for beauty lovers.
