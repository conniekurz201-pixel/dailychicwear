Nighttime Skincare Routine Guide

June 10, 2026 | 6 min read | Skincare

Your skin does most of its repair and regeneration while you sleep. A good nighttime routine can make all the difference in your skin's appearance.

Double Cleanse

Start with an oil-based cleanser to dissolve makeup and sunscreen, then follow with a water-based cleanser to remove impurities.

Apply Treatment Products

This is the time to use active ingredients like retinol, exfoliants, or serums. Apply them when your skin is still slightly damp.

Eye Cream

The delicate skin around your eyes needs special attention. Use a gentle eye cream to address dark circles, puffiness, and fine lines.

Hydrate and Moisturize

Finish with a rich moisturizer or facial oil to seal in all the products and keep your skin hydrated throughout the night.

Weekly Treatments

  • Face mask: 1-2 times per week
  • Chemical exfoliant: 2-3 times per week
  • Deep conditioning hair treatment

Final Tips

Be consistent with your routine, change your pillowcase regularly, and get enough sleep. Your skin will show the results!
