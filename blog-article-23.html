ASOS Review: Online Fashion for Every Style

June 20, 2026 | 10 min read | Brand Reviews

ASOS has become one of the most popular online fashion retailers, known for its extensive selection of trendy clothing at affordable prices. Founded in 2000, this British e-commerce giant has grown to serve customers in over 190 countries. In this review, we'll take a closer look at what ASOS has to offer.

About ASOS

ASOS (As Seen On Screen) is an online-only fashion retailer that offers a wide range of clothing, accessories, and beauty products for men and women. The company carries over 850 brands, including its own label, and is particularly popular among younger shoppers for its trendy and affordable offerings.

Overall Rating

★★★★☆
4.3/5

Based on 9,000+ customer reviews and our own testing

Pros of Shopping at ASOS

Vast Selection

ASOS offers over 850 brands and thousands of products, ensuring you'll find something for every style.

Affordable Prices

Trendy fashion at budget-friendly prices, with regular sales and discounts.

Inclusive Sizing

Offers sizes from petite to plus size, with dedicated sections for different body types.

Global Shipping

Ships to over 190 countries, making fashion accessible worldwide.

Cons of Shopping at ASOS

Quality Issues

Some items feel cheap and may not hold up well over time.

Sizing Inconsistency

Sizing can vary between brands and even within the same brand.

Returns Process

International returns can be complicated and expensive.

Shipping Times

International shipping can take longer than expected.

Best Categories at ASOS

ASOS excels in several categories:

Women's Fashion

ASOS offers a wide range of women's clothing:

  • Dresses: From casual to formal, ASOS has dresses for every occasion
  • Tops: Trendy blouses, t-shirts, and sweaters
  • Bottoms: Jeans, skirts, and trousers
  • Outerwear: Jackets, coats, and cardigans

Men's Fashion

ASOS also has a great selection for men:

  • Casual wear: T-shirts, hoodies, and jeans
  • Smart casual: Shirts, chinos, and blazers
  • Athletic wear: Tracksuits and sportswear

Accessories

Complete your look with ASOS accessories:

  • Bags: Crossbody, tote, and backpack options
  • Jewelry: Earrings, necklaces, and bracelets
  • Sunglasses: Trendy styles for every face shape

ASOS Own Brands

ASOS offers several exclusive brands:

  • ASOS Design: Affordable, trendy fashion for men and women
  • ASOS Petite: Fashion for shorter frames
  • ASOS Curve: Plus-size fashion
  • ASOS Tall: Fashion for taller individuals

Shopping Tips for ASOS

Here are some tips to make the most of your ASOS shopping experience:

  • Read reviews before buying, especially for sizing information
  • Sign up for ASOS Premier for free shipping and exclusive discounts
  • Check the sale section for great deals
  • Use the ASOS app for easy shopping on the go
  • Take advantage of student discounts if applicable

Final Verdict

ASOS is a great option for fashion-forward shoppers who want trendy clothing at affordable prices. With its vast selection and inclusive sizing, there's something for everyone. While the quality may not be premium, it's more than adequate for the price point. If you're comfortable with online shopping and understand the sizing challenges, ASOS is definitely worth checking out.
