H&M Review: Affordable Fashion for Everyone

June 17, 2026 | 10 min read | Brand Reviews

H&M is one of the world's largest fast fashion retailers, known for its trendy clothing at incredibly affordable prices. Founded in Sweden in 1947, H&M has grown to become a global phenomenon with stores in over 74 countries. But with so many fast fashion options available, is H&M still worth shopping at? Let's find out.

About H&M

H&M (Hennes & Mauritz) was founded by Erling Persson in Västerås, Sweden. Today, the company operates under several brands including H&M, COS, Monki, Weekday, and & Other Stories. H&M is committed to sustainability and has set ambitious goals to become climate-positive by 2040.

Overall Rating

★★★★☆
4.0/5

Based on 8,000+ customer reviews and our own testing

Pros of Shopping at H&M

Unbeatable Prices

H&M offers some of the most affordable fashion options on the market, making style accessible to everyone.

Trendy Designs

Like other fast fashion brands, H&M quickly adapts to the latest trends from the runway.

Sustainability Efforts

H&M has made significant strides in sustainability, including recycling programs and eco-friendly materials.

Wide Sizing

H&M offers inclusive sizing from XS to 4XL, catering to a wide range of body types.

Cons of Shopping at H&M

Quality Concerns

Some items feel cheap and don't hold up well over time or after multiple washes.

Inconsistent Sizing

Sizing can vary significantly between different items and collections.

Fast Fashion Impact

Despite sustainability efforts, H&M still contributes to the fast fashion cycle.

Hit-or-Miss Selection

With such a large inventory, not every item is a winner.

Best Categories at H&M

H&M excels in certain categories more than others:

Best for Basics

H&M is perfect for building a capsule wardrobe with basics like:

  • T-shirts and tank tops
  • Leggings and joggers
  • Basic dresses
  • Simple tops and blouses

Best for Trend Pieces

If you want to try a trend without committing a lot of money, H&M is great for:

  • Statement jackets
  • Printed dresses
  • Seasonal accessories
  • Occasion wear

Best for Kids

H&M has an excellent kids' section with:

  • Adorable designs
  • Durable play clothes
  • Affordable prices
  • Fun character-themed items

Sustainability at H&M

H&M has made significant commitments to sustainability:

  • Conscious Collection: Sustainable fashion line
  • Recycle Program: Bring old clothes for recycling
  • Circular Fashion: Working towards a circular economy
  • Eco-Friendly Materials: Using organic cotton, recycled polyester

Shopping Tips for H&M

Here are some tips to make the most of your H&M shopping experience:

  • Check the sale section first for great deals
  • Sign up for H&M Club rewards program
  • Read reviews before buying online
  • Consider sizing up, especially for dresses and tops
  • Look for the Conscious Collection for more sustainable options

Final Verdict

H&M is a great option for budget-conscious shoppers who want to stay on trend without breaking the bank. While the quality may not be premium, it's more than adequate for the price. If you're looking for basics, trend pieces, or kids' clothes, H&M is definitely worth checking out.
