Kiehl's Review: Timeless Skincare

June 25, 2026 | 11 min read | Brand Reviews

Kiehl's has been a trusted name in skincare for over 160 years, known for its effective, no-nonsense approach to beauty. Founded in 1851 as a pharmacy in New York City, Kiehl's has grown to become a global brand loved by skincare enthusiasts. In this review, we'll explore what makes Kiehl's stand the test of time.

About Kiehl's

Kiehl's was founded by John Kiehl, a German apothecary, in New York City. The brand remained a local pharmacy until the 1980s when it was acquired by L'Oreal. Today, Kiehl's is known for its high-quality skincare products that combine natural ingredients with scientific innovation.

Final Verdict

Kiehl's is a brand that truly delivers on its promise of effective skincare. With its no-nonsense approach and high-quality ingredients, Kiehl's has earned its reputation as a trusted skincare brand. While the price point may be higher than drugstore brands, the quality and results make it worth the investment for skincare enthusiasts.
