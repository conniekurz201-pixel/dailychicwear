Fredericks of Hollywood: The Iconic Lingerie Brand That Invented the Push-Up Bra

November 4, 2026 | 8 min read | Fashion

Ready for round 2? That's the headline lighting up Fredericks of Hollywood right now — and it's a fitting metaphor for a brand that's been reinventing lingerie since 1946. From inventing the push-up bra to putting black lace on the mainstream map, Fredericks isn't just a lingerie label. It's a piece of Hollywood history that's still making waves today.

A Hollywood Origin Story

Fredericks of Hollywood was born in the backroom of a famous Hollywood Boulevard lingerie shop, founded by Frederick Mellinger — an Austrian immigrant with a sharp eye for what women actually wanted. It was Mellinger who, in the 1940s, took the first step toward reshaping the industry: he designed and patented the world's first push-up bra. Before Fredericks, bras were mostly functional, utilitarian garments. Mellinger's padded, underwired creations gave women lift, shape, and confidence — and forever changed the silhouette of Hollywood glamour.

More Than Just a Bra Brand

The push-up bra is Fredericks' most famous invention, but it's far from the only one. Over seven decades, the brand has been responsible for:

  • Popularizing black lingerie — previously considered risqué, now a wardrobe staple
  • Introducing the balconette bra with its wide-set straps and flattering lift
  • Celebrating curves with plus-size collections that were ahead of their time
  • Blending fashion-forward design with accessible price points — most bras sit comfortably in the $40–$60 range

Why Fredericks of Hollywood Still Matters

In an era where new lingerie brands pop up every month, Fredericks stands out because it has something no startup can buy: authenticity. This is a brand that didn't just jump on a trend — it created the trends. When you slip into a Fredericks push-up, you're wearing a garment that's part of a 75-year story of female confidence, Hollywood glamour, and design innovation. And let's be real — not every brand can say they invented an item of clothing. Fredericks can. The push-up bra isn't just a product. It's proof that one person in a Hollywood backroom can change how millions of women feel about themselves every single day.
