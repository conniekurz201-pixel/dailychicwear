Ulta Beauty Review: The Best Drugstore to Luxury Beauty Destination

May 20, 2026 | 12 min read | Brand Reviews

Ulta Beauty has become a favorite among beauty enthusiasts for its unique blend of drugstore and luxury brands all under one roof. Founded in 1990, Ulta has grown to over 1,300 stores across the United States, offering an unparalleled shopping experience. In this review, we'll explore what makes Ulta stand out from the competition.

About Ulta Beauty

Ulta Beauty is an American beauty retailer that combines drugstore brands like Maybelline and L'Oreal with high-end luxury brands like Dior and Chanel. What sets Ulta apart is its "everything beauty" concept, offering cosmetics, skincare, haircare, fragrance, and even salon services in select locations.

Overall Rating

★★★★☆
4.5/5

Based on 7,500+ customer reviews and our own testing

Pros of Shopping at Ulta

Mix of Drugstore & Luxury

Ulta offers the best of both worlds - affordable drugstore finds and high-end luxury brands.

Ultamate Rewards Program

Earn points on every purchase that can be redeemed for discounts and free products.

In-Store Services

Many Ulta locations offer salon services, brow bars, and makeup applications.

Generous Return Policy

Return most products within 60 days, even if opened and used.

Cons of Shopping at Ulta

Limited International Presence

Ulta is primarily a US-based retailer, which means international shoppers miss out.

Stock Issues

Popular products often sell out quickly, especially during sales events.

Price Points

While they have affordable options, luxury brands can be pricey.

Store Layout

Some stores can feel cluttered and overwhelming.

Best Brands at Ulta

Ulta carries over 25,000 products from more than 500 brands. Here are some of our favorites:

Drugstore Favorites

  • Maybelline: Great drugstore makeup at unbeatable prices
  • L'Oreal: Innovative skincare and haircare
  • CeraVe: Dermatologist-recommended skincare
  • Neutrogena: Effective and affordable skincare

Luxury Brands

  • Estee Lauder: Timeless luxury skincare
  • MAC Cosmetics: Professional-quality makeup
  • Clinique: Allergy-tested skincare and makeup
  • Benefit: Fun and innovative makeup products

Ulta Exclusive Brands

  • IT Cosmetics: Cosmetics with skincare benefits
  • Urban Decay: Bold and edgy makeup
  • Too Faced: Sweet-scented, high-quality makeup

Shopping Tips for Ulta

Here are some tips to maximize your Ulta shopping experience:

  • Sign up for Ultamate Rewards to earn points
  • Take advantage of 21 Days of Beauty sales
  • Check the website for weekly deals and coupons
  • Use the "Try It On" feature on the app
  • Take advantage of free shipping on orders over $35

Ultamate Rewards Program

Ulta's rewards program is one of the best in the industry:

  • Member: Earn 1 point per $1 spent
  • Platinum: Earn 1.25 points per $1 spent (after $500 spent)
  • Diamond: Earn 1.5 points per $1 spent (after $1,000 spent)
  • Birthday Gift: Free gift during your birthday month

Final Verdict

Ulta Beauty offers a unique shopping experience that combines accessibility with luxury. Whether you're a budget-conscious beauty lover or someone who enjoys splurging on high-end products, Ulta has something for everyone. The rewards program, generous return policy, and wide selection make Ulta a must-visit destination for beauty enthusiasts.
